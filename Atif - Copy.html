Artist Profile

Atif Aslam

The Voice of South Asia

Atif Aslam is a globally acclaimed Pakistani playback singer, songwriter, and actor. Known for his powerful vocal belting technique and soulful melodies, he has recorded numerous chart-topping songs in Pakistan, India, and across the world.

Born: March 12, 1983 (Wazirabad, Pakistan)
Genres: Pop • Rock • Sufi • Filmi
Active Years: 2003 - Present
Honors: Tamgha-e-Imtiaz (Government of Pakistan)
